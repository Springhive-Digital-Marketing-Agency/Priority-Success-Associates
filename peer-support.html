You Don't Have to Walk the Journey Alone

Individualized support from trained peer professionals who use lived experience to encourage recovery, strengthen self-advocacy, and support personal goals.

What is Peer Support?

Peer Support Services provide an invaluable connection rooted in shared understanding. Our Peer Support Specialists are trained professionals who draw upon their own lived experiences with behavioral health, substance use, or life challenges to assist others on their path to recovery and wellness.

Because they have walked similar paths, our peer professionals offer a unique form of empathy, hope, and practical guidance that complements clinical therapy services.

How Our Peer Professionals Can Help

Our dedicated peer support team works alongside you to navigate daily challenges, build life skills, and connect you with the resources necessary for lasting success.

Encourage Recovery

Providing hope and motivation through shared experiences, demonstrating that meaningful recovery and positive change are entirely possible.

Build Self-Advocacy

Helping individuals find their voice, understand their rights, and effectively communicate their needs in various settings.

Identify Personal Goals

Collaboratively developing practical, achievable goals that align with what matters most to the individual.

Develop Independence

Teaching and reinforcing essential daily living skills, coping strategies, and problem-solving techniques.

Community Connection

Assisting individuals in identifying and accessing helpful community resources, support networks, and social opportunities.

Navigate Life Challenges

Providing a reliable, empathetic sounding board when facing obstacles, life transitions, or moments of crisis.
